Sai Pallavi Senthamarai Kannan is a popular Indian actress and dancer who mainly works in Telugu, Tamil and Malayalam films. She was born on 9 May 1992 in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. She completed her medical studies at Tbilisi State Medical University in Georgia. Sai Pallavi started her acting career with the Malayalam movie *Premam* in 2015, where she played the role of Malar. She made her Telugu debut with the movie *Fidaa* in 2017, which brought her great recognition among Telugu audiences. Sai Pallavi is well known for her natural acting, beautiful smile, expressive performances and excellent dancing skills. Some of her popular movies include *Premam, Fidaa, MCA, Padi Padi Leche Manasu, Love Story, Shyam Singha Roy, Gargi* and *Amaran*. She has received several awards for her performances in different languages. Her dedication, simplicity and talent have made her one of the well-known actresses in Indian cinema. She continues to inspire many young people through her hard work and passion for acting.
